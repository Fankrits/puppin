## Scope

Storefront homepage (`src/routes/$countryCode/index.tsx`) plus the shared chrome it sets: navbar, footer, product card, primary button, global tokens. Mode: Persuade.

## Surface strategy

- Visitor: a design-led Thai shopper meeting Puppin for the first time.
- Belief to leave with: cute brand, seriously good clothes.
- Action: add to cart in working form, in the hero, then browse the full grid.
- Proof on hand: the Puppin character (generated with agy from the character sheets) and the live Medusa catalog. No reviews, photos, or quality claims exist; do not invent them.
- Craft bar: sentry.io — a clean, conversion-first structure carried by confident display type and illustrated character moments with a sense of humour.
- Chosen: the category standard (canon), steered: Puppin in the hero, and the Label Room's full-measure condensed uppercase headings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar, clean own-label storefront where Puppin is the one doing the selling; refuses the anonymous template with a mascot stuck in the corner, and refuses a toy-store look.
OWN-WORLD: White page, ink #121212 type, Puppin red #D63A2C as the single action colour, pale sprout mint #C8E6D6 (matched to the generated hero render's backdrop) as the hero field. Full-measure condensed uppercase display (Anybody, Thai falls to Kanit), Anuphan for UI and body. Puppin appears in plush renders that peek, hug, measure and nap at section edges.
STORY: The visitor meets Puppin hugging the clothes, reads one confident line, adds a piece to the cart from the hero tag, then browses New In and leaves knowing it is an own label.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim white nav. Below it a rounded mint hero panel: the headline set full-measure across the panel's left 60% in huge condensed caps, Thai line beneath, then a hang-tag quick-add for the featured product (name, price, size chips, red Add to cart) at bottom-left; Puppin hugging folded tees fills the right 45%, overlapping the panel's bottom edge.
FORM: The category standard (canon), steered; position: canon exit, not on the ranked list; seed key b041cc40.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
